# Phase 15: AI Compliance Assistant

## Overview
Carefully integrated AI into the compliance workflow as an advisory and explanatory tool.

## Capabilities
- AI can summarize the current state of a compliance framework based on retrieved records.
- AI can explain why a specific piece of evidence satisfies a control.

## Strict Boundaries
- **No Certification Decisions:** AI MUST NOT mark a control as passed or claim that Sentrix AI is officially certified.
- **No Evidence Fabrication:** AI cannot invent evidence to satisfy a missing control. If evidence is missing, it responds "Insufficient evidence."
- **No Authorization Bypass:** AI cannot alter compliance states, change governance policies, or tamper with the audit logs.
