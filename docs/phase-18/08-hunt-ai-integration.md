# Phase 18: Safe AI Hunt Assistance

## Overview
Extended the AI Assistant to interpret threat hunt operations.

## Capabilities & Strict Boundaries
- **Capabilities:** AI can summarize a hunt's findings, synthesize analyst notes, and recommend structured JSONB queries based on natural language input.
- **Boundaries:** AI cannot execute hunts autonomously. AI cannot invent findings that do not exist in the database. AI cannot run shell commands, SQL, or arbitrary APIs.
