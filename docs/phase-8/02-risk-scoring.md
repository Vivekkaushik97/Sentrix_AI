# Phase 8: Risk Scoring

## Overview
The `UpiRiskScorer` guarantees a predictable, explainable risk mapping bounded strictly between 0 and 100.

## Deterministic Scoring Matrix
- **Detections**: Base score accumulates from specific rule findings. For instance, `UpiVelocityRule` adds a rigid +40 threat penalty to the score.
- **Heuristics**: Independent markers unassociated with a specific "Rule" (e.g. exceptionally large transfer amounts > ₹50,000) contribute minor risk points (+10).
- **Severity Mapping**:
  - `0-24`: LOW
  - `25-49`: MEDIUM
  - `50-74`: HIGH
  - `75-100`: CRITICAL

## Anti-Hallucination
No scores are generated by the LLM. The AI Assistant only receives the final Integer and contextual Reason, fulfilling its role as an explainer rather than a black-box arbiter of transactional safety.
